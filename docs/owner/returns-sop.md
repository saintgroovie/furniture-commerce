## Purpose

Tell managers how to classify an appeal and what they must not say. Statutory numbers here are **legal obligations**, not Woodright commercial promises.

This SOP does **not** choose: a longer voluntary return window; free reverse shipping for good-quality remote refuse; a dedicated claims email; automated refund.

## Sales modes (product vs law)

| Label | Product meaning | Legal effect on return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `STANDARD` | Catalog model as listed; cart | Not automatically returnable or banned. Remote good-quality refuse may apply (ст. 26.1) unless the **facts** meet the individually-determined test. |
| `CONFIGURABLE` | Preset finish / colour / fabric / listed size | Choosing a **pre-existing option** is **not** automatically «индивидуально-определённые свойства». Do not refuse on the label. |
| `BESPOKE` | Beyond presets; spec / sketch; no cart | Stronger *factual* candidate for the individually-determined exception. Still **not** automatic no-return. Defect rights (ст. 18–24) remain. |

Record in Счёт-заказ / specification when relevant: SKU; preset vs non-preset size; unique drawing; that the object can be used **only** by this buyer (if that is the agreed fact). The exception needs **provable facts**, not a nav label.

## Return of good-quality goods

Keep this flow **separate** from defects.

### Remote sale (typical site order)

Sale is remote when the consumer could not inspect the **specific** item or a sample at contract conclusion (ст. 26.1 п. 1). A website order shipped later is the default launch hypothesis. If the buyer inspected and bought a **specific displayed piece** in the showroom, use the offline row.

| Rule | Content | Authority | Public implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before transfer | Consumer may refuse **at any time** | ст. 26.1 п. 4 | Disclose as law, not a Woodright extra |
| After transfer | **7 days** if written return info was given at delivery | ст. 26.1 п. 4 | Same |
| After transfer, no written return info | **3 months** from transfer | ст. 26.1 п. 4 | Ops must give written return info at handover |
| Condition | Товарный вид and consumer properties preserved | ст. 26.1 п. 4 | Not a defect claim |
| Proof of purchase | Receipt helpful; **other proof allowed** | ст. 26.1 п. 4 | Never «без чека не примем» |
| Individually-determined | No refuse of **good-quality** goods that have individually-determined properties **and** can be used **exclusively** by this consumer | ст. 26.1 п. 4 | Facts + evidence; not BESPOKE label |
| Money back | **10 days** from the consumer’s demand, minus seller’s cost of shipping the returned goods **from the consumer** | ст. 26.1 п. 4 | `LEGAL OBLIGATION`, not `OD-06B` SLA |
| How to return (interim) | Until the statute is amended: any method **at the buyer’s choice** (remote or the seller’s stated method, or a method given on request) that lets the seller inspect on receipt, including carrier / postal. Buyer bears transit risk and transport cost | КС РФ 17.02.2026 N 7-П, operative §2 (RG 12.03.2026) | Do not require a personal visit as the only path |

КС 7-П struck п. 3–4 of ст. 26.1 **insofar as they omit a remote-return method**. It does **not** delete the 7-day / 3-month windows or the individually-determined exception.

ПП РФ от 31.12.2020 N 2463, перечень п. 8 «Мебельные гарнитуры бытового назначения»: Consultant note (and Роспотребнадзор information) - **this list is not applied to remote sale**. Do not use it to refuse a site-order good-quality return.

### Offline / showroom sale of a specific item

If the consumer inspected the item in the salon and bought it there (not a 26.1 remote contract):

- ст. 25: exchange of non-food goods of proper quality within **14 days excluding the purchase day**, if unused, appearance/labels preserved; receipt not the only proof.
- If no analogue is in stock, the consumer may refuse the contract and demand money (ст. 25).
- Перечень 2463 п. 8 blocks **exchange** of **мебельные гарнитуры бытового назначения**. It is **not** «all furniture». A single catalog piece is **not** automatically a гарнитур. Whether a room-set / multi-item order is a гарнитур is a **facts** question (`LEGAL REVIEW` on that order). Do not invent a universal furniture ban.
- Do **not** publish CS-Cart «14 дней» as Woodright remote policy.

### Individually determined properties (criterion)

Statute test (both limbs):

1. the **good-quality** item has individually-determined properties; **and**
2. it can be used **exclusively** by this consumer.

Safe applications (not production bans):

- Preset fabric / listed size / listed finish: **do not** treat as automatic exception.
- Size outside the listed row, unique drawing, one-off object made to a room: **candidate**; still need limb 2 and deal evidence.
- Defective goods: this exception **does not** remove ст. 18–24.

What to keep on the order: agreed non-standard dimensions; sketch annex; buyer confirmation that the specification is unique to their space / use.

## Defective goods

Do **not** run the 7-day «передумал» clock as the defect path.

Consumer **chooses** among ст. 18 remedies (unless a special regime applies): replacement with same / other model; price reduction; free repair or reimbursement of repair; refuse the contract and money back. Seller may require return of the defective goods **at seller’s expense**.

Do **not** tell the buyer that Woodright alone picks the remedy when the law gives the consumer the choice.

- During the **commercial warranty** (12 months): seller must show consumer / third-party / force-majeure cause to avoid liability (ст. 18 п. 6).
- After 12 months but within **2 years**: consumer may still claim if they prove pre-transfer cause (ст. 19 п. 5; ГК ст. 477 п. 5).
- Quality check: seller must accept the goods and may inspect; consumer may attend (ст. 18 п. 5). Photos help ops; they are **not** a statutory filing condition.
- Expertise: in a dispute on causes, seller pays for expertise (ст. 18 п. 5); if expertise shows no seller liability, consumer reimburses those costs.
- Large / >5 kg goods: delivery for repair, markdown, replacement, and return to the consumer is **seller’s** cost (ст. 18 п. 7).
- Refund of the price for a defect claim: **10 days** from the demand (ст. 22) - `LEGAL OBLIGATION`.
- Furniture is **not** treated as technically complex by default (15-day special rule). Do not apply that list unless the SKU is actually on it.

Defect reverse shipping: Роспотребнадзор information on Правила 2463 - return of **non-conforming** goods at seller’s expense (ops still arranges case-by-case).

## Warranty vs statutory rights

Commercial warranty (12 months, seller ООО «Роэл-Техник») is **additional**. It does not replace ст. 18–24. After month 12, do not say «прав больше нет». Detail: `docs/owner/warranty-public-policy.md`.

## Intake flow

Channel-neutral: **обратиться к менеджеру Woodright**. Use current canonical contacts in `apps/storefront/src/lib/showroom-contacts.ts` (showroom, 8-800, mobile, TG / WA / MAX). Do **not** invent email. Do **not** use `sales@demostore.ru`, `woodright.t@yandex.ru`, `order@woodright.com`, `Woodright.Grand@yandex.ru`. Messengers are a **reach** channel, not a ratified exclusive formal filing form (`LEGAL REVIEW` if someone treats a chat as the only valid claim).

1. Buyer contacts Woodright.
2. Manager records contact and **order number** if any.
3. Buyer describes the problem.
4. Photos / video **if possible** (not a statutory bar).
5. Manager registers the case.
6. Classify:
   - good-quality return / refuse (remote or offline);
   - defect / warranty / statutory claim;
   - service / non-warranty (paid).
7. Inspection / quality check if needed.
8. Tell the buyer the **next step** (not a fake SLA).

Visible damage at handover: record on the act (quantity, assortment, obvious defects). That does **not** wipe later claims for **hidden / manufacturing** defects. Do not say «не указали в акте - pretensions lost».

## Evidence / documents

May ask: order number; contract / Счёт-заказ; cheque; act; photos; other proof of purchase.

Must not refuse only because a paper cheque is missing (ст. 18 п. 5; ст. 26.1 п. 4; ст. 25). CTR-2026 «keep documents» is not a lawful automatic refuse.

---

## Inspection / quality check

Seller may inspect. Consumer may attend. DIY assembly damage vs independent manufacturing defect: see warranty policy. Do not void all warranty because the buyer lifted or assembled the item.

## Refund / replacement handling

PaymentLink has **no** refund API. Refunds are manual. Statutory 10-day clocks (26.1 and 22) remain legal duties. Same-rail vs bank transfer is ops, not a public promise. `OD-05` unpaid-order abandon vs contract formation stays `LEGAL REVIEW` for civil-law moment; still do not invent a cancellation fee.

## Internal decision tree

```text
CUSTOMER REQUEST
  |
  +-- defect / shortage / damage alleged?
  |     yes --> defect / warranty / statutory (ст. 18–24)
  |             do not apply 7-day «передумал» clock
  |
  +-- no defect (good quality)
        |
        +-- goods not yet transferred? --> remote: refuse allowed (26.1)
        |
        +-- transferred
              |
              +-- remote site sale? --> 26.1 windows + condition +
              |                         individually-determined test +
              |                         KS 7-П return method
              |
              +-- offline inspected piece? --> ст. 25 + перечень 2463 п. 8
                                               (гарнитур only; not all furniture)
```

Then, only as **facts**: individually-determined? furniture set? STANDARD / CONFIGURABLE / BESPOKE evidence on the order? Labels never auto-decide.

## What managers must not say

Legal verification supports these as **incorrect** or unlawfully broad:

- «мебель возврату не подлежит» (universal)
- «Bespoke вернуть нельзя при любых обстоятельствах»
- «без чека заявление не примем»
- «если сами собрали, гарантия сгорела»
- «после 12 месяцев никаких прав нет»
- «не указали в акте - pretensions невозможны»
- «без фото претензию не примем»
- «рассмотрим за 5 дней» as a Woodright promise
- Demo Magazin / 14 дней CS-Cart / PP 55 as current law
